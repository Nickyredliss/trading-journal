When you hit your goal or your loss limit, the broker page is covered and the buttons are gone. You can override it by holding a button for three seconds — and that day gets marked in your history, so you see how often you broke your own rule.

FAQ

Does it work with my broker?

Yes — with any web platform. You enable the panel on a site with one click; the extension does not need to know your broker. Automatic trade detection for popular platforms is coming later.

Can I turn the STOP off?

You can hold a button for three seconds to override it. We don't recommend it, and the day is marked as "overridden" in your history. A lock you can't override is planned.

Is it really free?

Yes, everything in the extension today is free with no limits. Paid extras (auto-detection, weekly stats, sync) may come later; the core will stay free.

Where is my data?

Only in your browser (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is sent anywhere. You can export to CSV or delete everything from the History page.
